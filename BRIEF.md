# The Open Shelf: BRIEF

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** Answers given by the user are marked (user). Everything else is an authored decision.

## Build notes
- The scroll-craft skill's engine, scripts and references were not present in this install (only SKILL.md), and no KIE key is set. So the page is hand-built: HTML/CSS/JS, no generated photography. Book covers and room are drawn in CSS. Fingerprint registry not available.

## The eight topics
1. Vibe (user): immersive yet simple, welcoming. References (user): press.stripe.com (a catalogue that reads like a library, not a funnel), quadplex80.com (a calm entry gate with a consent-style choice).
2. Journey: threshold, the idea, the shelf, how the money moves, for authors, a hold at the end. (authored)
3. Energy: calm open, one lift at the shelf, calm explanation, quiet close. (authored)
4. Feeling: welcomed, then curious, then trusting, then invited. Peak: the shelf. (authored)
5. Signature move: **the library slip**. Clicking a book pulls a due-date slip from the book, stamped with the author's own web address, and hands the reader off to that author. It shows the whole business in one gesture: no middle shelf. (authored)
6. Aesthetic (user): editorial. Warm paper, serif display, generous space.
7. Structure (user): distinct scenes, like rooms in a bookshop.
8. Assets: none. Sample authors and books are fictional and labelled as samples.
- Audience (user): both readers and authors, split from the first screen.
- Name (authored): The Open Shelf.

## Grammar
Rooms: separate scenes with different behaviour, no continuous camera. Devices: parallax (hero), pin + kinetic text, lateral pan (shelf), scrub (money diagram), clip-path reveal (authors), pointer (close).

## Feeling curve
1. Threshold: welcomed. Warm light, layered shelves moving at different depths.
2. The idea: calm certainty. One line at a time on a still page.
3. The shelf (PEAK): delight. Lateral travel past covers, hover lifts a book, click triggers the slip. Largest scroll span.
4. Money: trust. A line draws from reader to author, plainly.
5. For authors: invitation. A dark room opens as a circular wipe.
6. Close: resolved. One book that follows the pointer, two clear actions.

**Peak sentence:** "I clicked a book and it stamped the author's own website on a library slip."
**Tell-someone:** "It's the site where the bookshop hands you straight to the author."
**Authored silence:** the idea scene holds still on purpose between lines.

## Revision 2: sky world and ebook marketplace
- Model (user): authors choose to sell ebooks through the shelf (we take payment, deliver, pay them the larger share) or link readers to their own site. Differentiator (user): authors receive the buyer's email (with buyer opt-in), the majority of the sale, and marketing help.
- Design (user): drop the typical cream/terracotta/serif-plus-Inter look. Immersive, more movement, like quadplex80's cloud gate. Direction chosen: soft sky world.
- Assets: kie.ai nano-banana-2 stills (dawn, day, high morning, dusk, night plus a portrait dawn), kling/v2-1-pro 5s clips (dawn flight, cloud flight) encoded all-intra-ish for scrubbing.
- Design system: Bodoni Moda (headlines, italic accents) + Figtree. Sky palette by time of day, one orange sun accent, sharp 2px corners, no pills or glass. Chrome: wordmark, text-link nav, a time-of-day altitude gauge instead of a counter.
- Grammar: rooms by time of day. Dawn (scrub flight + drifting covers), Morning (kinetic promises), Day (lateral pan shelf, PEAK), Noon (second scrub flight + money steps), Dusk (circular reveal for authors), Night (pointer lantern + stars).
- Signature move: the boarding pass. Clicking an ebook opens a pass with an unticked "share my email with the author" box that changes what the author receives, then stamps SENT.
- Feeling curve: welcomed (dawn), calm certainty (morning), delight (day, peak), trust (noon), invitation (dusk), resolve (night).
- Entry gate with "Enter without motion" option.
