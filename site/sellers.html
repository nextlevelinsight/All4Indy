How it works

  1. Apply. Tell us about you and your book. Takes a few minutes.
  2. Verify. We confirm who you are and set up payouts through our payments partner.
  3. Sign the agreement. Fees, referral terms and disclosures, written in plain language. You can read it before you apply.
  4. List your book. Upload your EPUB, cover and sample chapter. Or add a link to your own store. Add your newsletter link.
  5. Get found and get paid. We help market it. Sales show in your dashboard within minutes, and payouts arrive monthly.

Fees, in the open

Sell through us

We take payment, deliver the ebook and pay you. You see every fee on every sale.

  • Platform fee: 10% of the sale price (5% for founding authors, first year)
  • Card processing: passed through at cost
  • Tax: collected and remitted as set out in the agreement

Send readers to you

You sell on your own site, including paperbacks. We link to you.

  • Referral fee: 5 to 8% of verified referred orders, or a flat monthly listing fee
  • Billed monthly, shown in your statement
  • You keep 100% of the sale itself

1. Parties

This agreement is between [Company Legal Name] ("the Shelf") and [Author Legal Name] ("the Author").

2. What the Author provides

The Author lists one or more books, and confirms they own or control the rights to each. The Author grants the Shelf a non-exclusive licence to host, display, market and deliver the listed books to readers. The Author keeps all other rights, and may sell elsewhere.

3. Sell-through fees

For books sold through the Shelf, the Shelf keeps a platform fee of [10]% of the sale price, plus card processing costs at cost. The remainder is the Author's. Founding authors pay [5]% for [12] months from signing.

4. Referral fee for link-outs

Where the Author sells on their own site and the Shelf refers the reader, the Author pays the Shelf a referral fee of [5 to 8]% of the sale price of verified referred orders (or a flat fee of [$__] per month where agreed). A referred order means a purchase completed within [30] days of a reader following the Shelf's tracked link, as shown by [coupon code / order webhook / author statement]. The Shelf bills monthly. The Author may dispute a charge within [30] days.

5. Payouts

Sale proceeds are held for [14 to 30] days for refunds, then become available. Payouts are made monthly once the available balance reaches [$__], through the Shelf's payments partner. The Author must complete identity and tax verification before the first payout.

6. Refunds and chargebacks

If a sale is refunded or reversed, the amount is deducted from the Author's balance. Refund policy for readers: [state policy].

7. Taxes

[The Shelf collects and remits sales tax and VAT on sales it makes as seller / a merchant of record does so.] The Author is responsible for their own income tax and will provide the tax forms requested.

8. Reader ownership

Readers who buy through the Shelf receive a DRM-free copy and may re-download it from their library without time limit. The Author agrees this right survives termination of this agreement for copies already sold.

9. Reader data and newsletters

The Shelf does not share reader email addresses with the Author. The Shelf will show the Author's newsletter sign-up link to every buyer; readers join on the Author's own page. The Shelf may email readers its own news if they opted in. The Author will handle any list they build under applicable law.

10. Content and AI disclosure

The Author will not list unlawful, infringing or deceptive material, and will disclose if AI tools were used to generate the text, cover or narration. The Shelf may remove a listing that breaks these rules, after notice where reasonable.

11. Reporting

The Shelf provides a seller dashboard showing sales, fees, refunds, balances and payouts, updated [within minutes] of each event, and a monthly statement.

12. Term and ending this agreement

13. Liability and law

[Liability limits.] This agreement is governed by the laws of [jurisdiction].

Plain-language disclosures

  • Fees: every sale shows the card fee, our fee and your net. No hidden charges.
  • Timing: funds are held [14 to 30] days for refunds, then paid monthly.
  • Data: we keep reader emails for receipts and downloads. We do not give them to you. Readers can join your list through your link.
  • Tracking: link-out referrals are measured with tracked links. We will explain how in your statement.
  • AI: disclose AI use. Listings may be removed if misleading.
  • Not a guarantee: we help market your book but cannot promise sales.
